Founded by dedicated naturists in 1995, Sun Eden is now in its third decade of ever growing existence. Experience the exhilarating freedom of being yourself, in nature, without judgement or prejudice. Bask in the sun along the riverside or relax in the shade of our Bushveld trees. The day-to-day pressures of modern society are easily forgotten in this setting of natural beauty and tranquillity. Enjoy the friendly atmosphere while sitting around the campfire. Firewood and logs are supplied. Bring your own meat and marshmallows.
